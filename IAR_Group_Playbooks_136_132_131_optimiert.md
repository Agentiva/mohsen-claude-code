# Playbook 136 — Precision Metal Forming

`Language: en`

## === Product Description ===

IAR Group is a Swiss-headquartered industrial automation and robotics group that builds automated inspection, testing and sorting systems for series manufacturing. The group brings together Bachmann Engineering, founded in 1972 in Zofingen, Switzerland, for turnkey robot and special machine construction, Sontec, founded in 1990, for testing, measurement and industrial image processing, Zeugma TSI, founded in 1997 in Mafra, Portugal, for industrial machinery and process automation, IAR Deutschland in Germany, and Automation Specialist Services in Huntley, Illinois for the North American market. Engineering, commissioning and aftermarket are delivered locally from Switzerland, Germany, Portugal, Spain and the United States.

For precision metal forming, IAR Group supplies automated inline inspection and sorting systems that inspect every single part instead of a sample. Parts are fed onto a continuously rotating glass rotary table and captured from every angle by multiple cameras with resolutions up to 20 megapixels, at throughputs of up to 420 parts per minute. The system measures critical dimensions, detects burrs, cracks, forming defects and surface flaws in the same pass, sorts against up to five separate criteria and packs conforming parts directly into trays. Every part is logged, which turns 100 percent inspection into auditable documentation for IATF 16949, PPAP and customer-specific quality agreements.

What differentiates IAR Group is that it builds the machines itself rather than integrating third-party equipment. Machine construction, vision technology and process automation sit inside the same group, which is why systems are configured around a customer's actual part geometry and press line instead of around a standard catalogue cell. Combined with more than fifty years of special machine building at Bachmann Engineering and engineering teams in five countries, this gives stampers a supplier who understands forming processes and can service the installed system from their own region.

INDUSTRY: Precision metal stamping, Deep drawing, Cold forming, Fineblanking, Spring manufacturing, Wire forming, Automotive components, Medical device components, Electrical components

USPs: 1. 100 percent inline inspection of every part at up to 420 parts per minute, replacing sample gauging and manual visual checks. 2. 360-degree capture on a continuously rotating glass rotary table, so top, bottom and flank are inspected in a single pass. 3. Dimensional measurement and surface defect detection combined in one system instead of two separate stations. 4. Automatic sorting against up to five criteria with direct tray packing for shipment. 5. Complete per-part data logging for IATF 16949 and PPAP evidence. 6. Systems are built in-house by the group, not integrated from third-party cells, so they are matched to the customer's part and line. 7. Over fifty years of special machine construction combined with a dedicated industrial image processing unit. 8. Local engineering and aftermarket from Switzerland, Germany, Portugal, Spain and the United States.

## === Value Proposition ===

IAR Group enables precision metal forming manufacturers to guarantee 100 percent outgoing quality without slowing down their presses. Automated inline inspection at up to 420 parts per minute replaces manual visual checks, sample gauging and expensive third-party sorting after quality escapes, while per-part data logging delivers the traceability that automotive and medical customers demand under IATF 16949 and PPAP. Because IAR Group builds the systems itself and services them from engineering sites in Switzerland, Germany, Portugal, Spain and the United States, stampers get a system matched to their actual part geometry and a support partner in their own region.

## === Use Cases (5) ===

**Inline inspection for high-speed progressive stamping**
Progressive stamping lines produce parts faster than any inspector can look at them, which forces manufacturers to fall back on sampling and hope the gaps stay empty. IAR Group integrates its inspection system directly into the line, capturing every part from all sides on a rotating glass table at up to 420 parts per minute and diverting rejects automatically. The manual inspection bottleneck disappears and every part that leaves the line has been measured.

**Defect detection and sorting for deep drawn components**
Deep drawing produces complex geometries where wrinkles, thinning and micro-cracks are easy to miss and expensive to discover at the customer. IAR Group inspects dimensional features and surfaces immediately after forming and diverts out-of-tolerance parts before they reach the next operation. Complaints drop and the recurring cost of external sorting services after an escape disappears.

**Audit-ready traceability for automotive and medical customers**
Automotive and medical customers increasingly write 100 percent inspection and full traceability into supply agreements, and demonstrating compliance with sampling records is a losing argument in an audit. IAR Group logs measurement data for every individual part, producing a complete and auditable record. Suppliers can evidence IATF 16949 and PPAP requirements with data instead of procedure documents.

**Spring and wire form inspection with automatic tray packing**
Compression, extension and torsion springs as well as wire formed and multi-slide parts are traditionally checked by hand gauging, which is slow, inconsistent and impossible at volume. IAR Group verifies critical dimensions and detects material defects automatically, then sorts and packs conforming parts into trays ready for shipment. Inspection cost per part falls and the packing step is absorbed into the same cell.

**Bringing third-party sorting back in house**
After a quality incident, many stampers end up paying external sorting providers month after month, an operating cost that solves nothing permanently. IAR Group replaces that recurring spend with an owned inspection and sorting system that runs continuously. The recurring fee becomes a one-time investment, and outgoing quality stops depending on how carefully a contractor worked that week.
